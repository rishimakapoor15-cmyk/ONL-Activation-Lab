Value Chain Analysis → targeted nudges

A two-week Account-State Census finds the exact step each account is stuck on. That step decides the nudge (at most 2 a month, salary-day timing only with consent). Order Abandonment Rescue is the nudge at the last step: it catches an exited order ticket, asks why, and routes to matching help.

Next three bets after the 12-week pilot

Funded from the scale budget only; not counted in the +206

Each is built only if the week-12 evidence points to the bottleneck it solves.

Seamless Bank–ONL Experience

Secure navigation between the bank app and ONL, preserving onboarding progress, to remove the second login.

Build only ifLogin is the biggest measured drop-off in the census and pilot (checked at week 9).
Relation to the pilotExtends the integration MVP (card, deep link, readiness page) into single sign-on.
NeedsToken-based authentication, data-sharing consent, a security test and releases in both apps (8-12+ weeks).
MeasureLogin drop-off rate; first-trade conversion
2

ONL Investment Discovery Hub

Explore market themes, compare securities and build watchlists using company fundamentals and risk information, with no recommendations.

Build only ifRescue data shows "don't know what to buy" or many customers choosing "just explore".
Relation to the pilotSits before the order ticket; the Bridge starts only once a customer has chosen a security.
NeedsContent governance and advertisement-code review; no ranking or suggestion of securities.
MeasureWatchlist creation; first-trade conversion
3

Tailored Learning Journeys

Beginners learn order basics, charges and risks; experienced users go straight to ordering. Guidance without stock picking.

Build only ifMost Rescue exits cite unclear charges or confusing order fields.
Relation to the pilotExtends the Bridge's in-flow explanations into a library customers choose to open.
NeedsEducation rules (no recent price data), segment-level content, consent for personalisation.
MeasureFirst-trade conversion by segment
